## 2. What We Don't Process

In the free version, we **do not** process any personal data on our servers. **No registration** and **no sign-in** are required.

Specifically:

- **No user account** is created
- **No email address**, **no password**, **no nickname** is stored
- **No cloud sync** takes place
- **No data exchange with co-travelers**

## 3. Local Data Processing on Your Device

All content you enter in the app (vacations, travel plans, trips, budget items, bucket-list items, prep tasks, packing lists, travel documents, accommodations, journal entries, voice transcripts, photos, locations) is stored exclusively **locally on your device**. This data does not leave the device and is not accessible to us.

No processing within the meaning of the GDPR by us takes place.

## 4. Apple Services

The following Apple services are used in the background without any personal data being transferred to us:

- **WeatherKit** — displays weather data for your destinations. Apple processes the transmitted coordinates according to its own privacy policy.
- **MapKit** — displays maps and enables location search. Search queries are processed by Apple.
- **Push Notifications** — generated locally on your device and not sent via Apple's push servers.

Apple's [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) applies in addition.

## 5. Photo Library

If you add photos to your vacations, iOS asks you for permission to access the photo library. The selected images are stored locally in the app database and are not transferred to us.

## 6. Voice Memos and Speech Recognition

When you record a voice memo in the journal, the app uses Apple's on-device **Speech framework** with enforced on-device recognition (`requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true`). As a result:

- the recorded audio never leaves your device,
- no audio is persisted — only the resulting text transcript stays locally in your journal entry.

Using this feature requires one-time permission for microphone and speech recognition. You can revoke it at any time in iOS Settings.

## 7. Apple Intelligence (FoundationModels)

If your device supports Apple Intelligence, the app can offer optional AI suggestions (journal drafts, trip ideas, packing lists, travel-document categorization). Processing runs **exclusively on your device** through Apple's **FoundationModels** framework. Only text data you have entered is passed to the model; no photos, no biometric data. Neither the inputs nor the suggestions leave your device.

## 8. Siri and Shortcuts

The app integrates Siri via **App Intents**. Actions like "Next vacation", "Open today's plan", and "Add expense" only access trip data stored locally in the on-device App Group container. No additional trip data is transmitted to us or to Apple servers; voice requests to Siri itself are subject to Apple's own privacy policy.

## 9. Local Notifications

The app schedules local push notifications (pre-trip countdown, anniversary reminder, evening journal prompt) directly on your device. They are not delivered through servers and can be disabled at any time in the app's settings or in iOS system settings.

## 10. Your Rights

Since we do not process personal data in the free version, the GDPR subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, etc.) are not practically relevant against us. You can:

- Delete all stored data at any time via **Settings → Data → Delete all local data**
- Uninstall the app at any time — this irretrievably deletes all local data
- File a **complaint** with a data protection authority

Authority responsible for us: Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit (BlnBDI)

## 11. Upgrade to Pro

If you upgrade the app to the Pro version through a **monthly auto-renewing subscription** (product ID `app.vacationdiary.pro.monthly`), an **extended privacy policy** applies because an account is created and cloud sync, travel groups, photo backup, and push notifications for assigned tasks are then activated. You will see this in the app after purchasing the subscription.

**Payment processing**

Purchase, renewal, and billing of the subscription are handled by Apple via the App Store. From Apple we only receive an anonymous entitlement status ("subscription active / not active") — no payment data, no billing address, no credit-card details, no Apple ID.

**Renewal and cancellation**

The subscription automatically renews for another month unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage and cancel your subscription at any time in your Apple account's **App Store settings**. After the paid period expires, access to Pro features ends and the app continues to be usable in the Free version.
