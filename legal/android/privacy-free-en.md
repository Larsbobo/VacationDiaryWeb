## 2. What We Don't Process

In the free version, we **do not** process any personal data on our servers. **No registration** and **no sign-in** are required.

Specifically:

- **No user account** is created
- **No email address**, **no password**, **no nickname** is stored
- **No cloud sync** takes place
- **No data exchange with co-travelers**

## 3. Local Data Processing on Your Device

All content you enter in the app (vacations, travel plans, trips, budget items, bucket-list items, prep tasks, packing lists, travel documents, accommodations, journal entries, voice transcripts, photos, locations) is stored exclusively **locally on your device**. This data is not transferred to us and is not accessible to us.

If you have enabled Android backup on your device, this local data may be part of the backup in your Google account. You set up the backup yourself in Android Settings; it is subject to Google's terms.

## 4. External Services

For maps, weather, routes, place search, and exchange rates, the app queries public services directly from your device. For technical reasons they receive your **IP address** and the requested **coordinates, search terms, or currency codes** — but **no other content of your trips**. No data is transferred to us in the process.

- **OpenStreetMap Foundation** (United Kingdom) — map tiles of the standard map as well as place search and reverse geocoding (Nominatim). [osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy)
- **Esri** (Environmental Systems Research Institute, Inc., USA) — satellite imagery and labels when you choose the "Satellite" or "Hybrid" map style in Settings.
- **Open-Meteo** (open-meteo.com) — weather forecasts for the coordinates of your destination.
- **Project OSRM** (router.project-osrm.org) — travel times and routes between the trips of a day in the Today view.
- **ExchangeRate-API** (open.er-api.com) — current exchange rates for your home currency.
- **Google** — Android's system geocoding service determines the country and local currency from your destination's coordinates; on devices with Google services, the coordinates are sent to Google for this. The app also checks the status of your purchases with Google Play (see Section 11).

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (providing the features you use). When satellite imagery is loaded (Esri) and with Google services, a transfer to a third country, in particular the USA, cannot be ruled out. For the United Kingdom, an adequacy decision of the EU Commission exists. [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies in addition.

We do **not** access your device location; the app does not request a location permission.

## 5. Photos

You pick photos via Android's photo picker; the app only gets access to the images you select. The selected images are stored locally in the app and are not transferred to us. If a photo contains its capture location in its metadata, the app reads it (Android asks for the "Access media location" permission for this) to show the photo on the photo map.

## 6. Voice Memos and Speech Recognition

When you record a voice memo in the journal, the app exclusively uses **Android's on-device speech recognition**. If your device offers no on-device recognition, the feature is disabled — the app does not fall back to an online service. As a result:

- the recorded audio never leaves your device,
- no audio is persisted — only the resulting text transcript stays locally in your journal entry.

Using this feature requires permission for the microphone. You can revoke it at any time in Android Settings.

## 7. On-Device AI Suggestions (Gemini Nano)

If your device supports the on-device language model **Gemini Nano**, the app can offer optional AI suggestions (journal drafts, trip ideas, packing lists, travel-document categorization). Processing runs **exclusively on your device** through the Android system component AICore (interface: Google's ML Kit GenAI). Only text data you have entered is passed to the model; no photos, no biometric data. Neither the inputs nor the suggestions leave your device.

## 8. App Shortcuts and Widget

A long press on the app icon offers shortcuts ("Today", "Add expense", "Next vacation") that only open the corresponding view. The home-screen widget shows a countdown or today's plan; the data it needs is stored locally in the app's storage. No data is transferred to us or third parties in the process.

## 9. Local Notifications

The app schedules local notifications (pre-trip countdown, anniversary reminder, evening journal prompt) directly on your device. They are not delivered through servers and can be disabled at any time in the app's settings or in Android system settings.

## 10. Your Rights

Since we do not process personal data on our servers in the free version, the GDPR subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, etc.) are not practically relevant against us. You can:

- Delete all stored data at any time via **Settings → Data → Delete all local data**
- Uninstall the app at any time — this irretrievably deletes all local data
- File a **complaint** with a data protection authority

## 11. Upgrade to Pro

If you upgrade the app to the Pro version through an **auto-renewing subscription** (monthly, every three months, or yearly; product ID `app.vacationdiary.pro.monthly`), an **extended privacy policy** applies because an account is created and cloud sync, travel groups, photo backup, and notifications for assigned tasks are then activated. You will see it in the app after purchasing the subscription.

**Payment processing**

Purchase, renewal, and billing of the subscription are handled by Google via Google Play. The app only receives information about the purchase status ("subscription active / not active"), which is only used on your device. We receive no payment data, no billing address, no credit-card details, and no details of your Google account.

**Renewal and cancellation**

The subscription automatically renews for the selected term (1 month, 3 months, or 1 year) unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage and cancel your subscription at any time in the **Google Play Store** under *Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions*. After the paid period expires, access to Pro features ends and the app continues to be usable in the Free version.
